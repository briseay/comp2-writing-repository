Research is a conversation.

Welcome! These resources are designed to help you move through the research process from beginning to end: finding and evaluating scholarly sources, understanding the conversation surrounding your topic, developing your own ideas, using evidence effectively, and documenting your research responsibly.

Research Find and understand the conversation.
Write Develop your own ideas from what you learn.
Apply Use those skills in major assignments.
Document Give sources accurate and responsible credit.
